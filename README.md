The lead story is the collapse of the COP31 climate talks in Antalya, ending with no deal after about 48 hours of overtime — the first full collapse of a COP's main agreement since Copenhagen 2009. The other five stories are the Democrats clinching 258 House seats, the largest majority since 1992; Ukraine sinking the Russian corvette Merkury off Libya; the Russian strike on Dymer that killed 11, with a seven-year-old boy pulled alive from the rubble; Carlos Alcaraz winning his maiden ATP Finals title in Turin; and the updated toll of 24 dead in the Cikande candy-factory fire in Indonesia.
